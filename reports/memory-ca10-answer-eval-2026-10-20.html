Reproduce and limits

See the runnable procedure and budget contract, versioned results, manifests and per-case provenance, and pinned vector replay cache. The latter avoids repeating embedding work. Both local containers and the CLI worker are removed; no production memory corpus changed.

  • Initial 15-case corpus/labels were new; frozen core labels/score did not change after any answer calls. Read-only methodology review corrected draft labels before the first CLI answer. Retrieval-only draft diagnostics were inspected.
  • Messages protocol chosen after six CLI outputs existed to fix observed token overhead; same labels/packets, no policy/prompt tuning. It is an observed diagnostic replicate, not an untouched end-to-end holdout.
  • One stochastic answer per condition, author-created synthetic facts and a disclosed closed scope vocabulary; no generalization, semantic-judge or speed-improvement claim. Interventions have small selected positive subsets and no paired negative expansion.
  • Three negative baseline cases all abstained; four positive baseline misses also answered null when evidence was absent/incomplete. The latter are end-to-end missed facts, not observed hallucinations.
  • One strict citation flag is manually disputed and retained in primary metrics. Summary rollout remains deferred even under the favorable adjudication because its median-input gate failed.
  • Seven-day capture query truncation is missing for all 15 enabled Pai captures, not observed as zero. OPS-40 audit clipping is at 1000 characters, distinct from the embedding byte bound. Of 41 fully retained hybrid-read queries, one exceeded 1024 UTF-8 bytes; this does not establish answer harm. Pin exclusion relevance remains unknown.
  • All corpus storage was disposable local PostgreSQL; synthetic vectors were captured from the pinned live model. No production persona memories were added or rewritten for CA-10. The CLI worker was removed; its six run/audit records survive. The temporary proxy forward was closed.
